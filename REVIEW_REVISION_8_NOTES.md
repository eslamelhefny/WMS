# Prototype V5 — Review Revision 8 Notes

Revision 8 refines the application identity and adds a dedicated Instructor workspace while preserving the complete Revision 7 lifecycle and Position-driven rules.

## Compact top bar

- The large Andalusia Academy logo block is removed from the sidebar.
- The sidebar watermark is disabled so the navigation remains clean and focused.
- A compact Andalusia Academy emblem and wordmark now appear in the top bar.
- The desktop top bar is 52 px high at both 1920×1080 and 1280×720.
- Search, theme, storage, notifications, Today's Tasks, and Add Task remain available with responsive labels and spacing.
- The top bar and page content do not create horizontal page overflow at either validated desktop size.

## Instructors sidebar workspace

- A new **Instructors** item appears directly after Programs in the sidebar.
- Instructor records are built from Stage 3 Instructor Setup and the active Instructor master-data directory.
- The table includes Instructor, Program / role, R/H, Project Based, Part / Full Time, Level, Assigned Groups, Internal / External, fee, contract, onboarding, status, and direct Program access.
- Search covers instructor, Program, role, level, group, source, engagement model, and status.
- Filters support All Programs, a specific Program, and Directory Only.
- Summary cards show unique instructors, selected instructors, external instructors, and ready/onboarded instructors.
- Opening a Program from the Instructor workspace goes directly to **Instructor Setup**.

## Preserved behavior

- Academy Manager and Program Operations Executive Program creation rules remain unchanged.
- Academy Manager full-stage authority remains unchanged.
- Save Draft and Submit for Greenlight remain separate actions.
- The eight-stage Academy lifecycle and every stage validation remain unchanged.
- Finance, Schedule, manager Launch Planning, crew-task controls, Planner, My Tasks, Documents, Reports, History, Training, Administration, and Profile remain available according to Position rules.
