## Academy Manager decision workflow

- The Academy Manager Dashboard shows every Program's complete Launch Planning record in one decision table.
- The table includes Go and No Go actions.
- No Go requires a reason and creates corrective work for Program Operations Executive.
- Go is disabled until every required Stage 4 control is complete.
- Go completes Launch Planning and activates Commercial.
