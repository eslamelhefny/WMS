# Prototype V5 — Review Revision 4 Notes

## Finance moved into Stage 4

Stage 4 is now named **Launch, Commercial, Operations & Finance** and contains four controlled sections:

- Section 4A — Launch Planning
- Section 4B — Commercial
- Section 4C — Operational Activities
- Section 4D — Finance

## Stage 4 handovers

1. Academy Manager Go unlocks Commercial.
2. Completed applicable commercial routes unlock Operational Activities.
3. Academy Operations Supervisor approval unlocks Finance.
4. Completed PMB and Treasury Finance controls complete Stage 4 and unlock Readiness.

## Finance governance preserved

- Finance — PMB owns actual revenue, actual cost, and final P&L closure.
- Finance — Treasury owns sponsorship collection, instructor/vendor settlements, and payment evidence.
- Position-specific assignments and personal workflow-task generation remain in place.

## Revised lifecycle

The user-facing lifecycle now contains eight stages. Graduate Outcomes, Reporting & Improvement, and Final Closure are renumbered as Stages 6–8.

Existing 13-stage and Revision 3 records are normalized when loaded. Standalone Finance records and assignments are mapped into the new combined Stage 4.
