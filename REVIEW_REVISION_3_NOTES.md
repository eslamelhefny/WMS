# Prototype V5 — Review Revision 3 Notes

## Academy Manager crew work dashboard

- The Academy Manager can create a task for any Academy Position.
- Tasks may be general Academy work or linked to a Program and lifecycle stage.
- Due date, priority, notes, assigned Position, and assigning manager are stored.
- The dashboard shows all crew assignments and total, open, overdue, and completed counts.
- When the assigned Position is active, the assignment generates its linked personal workflow task.

## Combined Stage 4

**Launch, Commercial & Operations** combines the former Stages 4, 5, and 6.

- Section 4A — Launch Planning
- Section 4B — Commercial
- Section 4C — Operational Activities

Academy Manager Go unlocks Commercial. Completing every applicable commercial route unlocks Operational Activities. Academy Operations Supervisor approval completes the combined stage.

## Combined Stage 5

**Readiness, Delivery & Assessment** combines the former Stages 7, 8, and 9.

- Section 5A — Readiness
- Section 5B — Delivery
- Section 5C — Assessment & Certification

Go or Conditional Go unlocks Delivery. Completed Delivery with no open incidents unlocks Assessment. Valid assessment and certificate evidence completes the combined stage and activates Finance.

## Compatibility

Existing local records using the former 13-stage structure are normalized to the revised nine-stage lifecycle when loaded. Existing assignments, tasks, and document stage references are mapped to the relevant combined stage.
