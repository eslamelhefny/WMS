# Prototype V5 — Review Revision 5 Notes

## Requested review change

The **Academy Manager can complete all stages** in the Academy Training Life Cycle.

## Manager authority added

- All Position-owned stage fields and actions are available when Academy Manager is active.
- The Program Workspace shows a clear **Academy Manager Authority** notice.
- The manager can complete open assignments in the selected stage's **Stage Work** panel.
- The manager can perform Curriculum draft, QA, and release actions.
- The manager can complete all Instructor Setup responsibilities.
- The manager can complete Launch, Commercial, Operational Activities, PMB Finance, and Treasury Finance responsibilities inside Stage 4.
- The manager can prepare Readiness, record the Go / Conditional Go / No-Go decision, complete Delivery, add or resolve incident work, and complete Assessment.
- The manager can complete Graduate Outcomes and Reporting & Improvement work.
- Final Closure remains an Academy Manager decision.

## Navigation and manager dashboard updates

- Weekly, Monthly, and Quarterly Planner links are consolidated into one sidebar **Planner** entry with internal tabs.
- Sidebar **Finance** provides an all-Program finance portfolio with PMB and Treasury information.
- Sidebar **Schedule** provides today's and planned timed delivery sessions.
- Session date and time are editable in the Delivery section and feed the Schedule automatically.
- Launch Planning and Academy Crew Tasks show five rows by default, with **See All / Show 5** controls.
- Delivery and Assessment remain combined inside **Stage 5 — Readiness, Delivery & Assessment**, satisfying the requested Stage 8/9 consolidation within the current eight-stage lifecycle.

## Governance retained

- Specialist Position owners remain visible on stages and assignments.
- Other Positions keep their original access boundaries.
- Required fields, evidence, sequence gates, financial checks, and count validation still apply.
- Completing a Stage Work assignment records completion in the audit and updates linked follow-up, incident, or improvement records.
